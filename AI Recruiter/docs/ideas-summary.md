# Sedna SAP × AI — All 11 Ideas (Reference)

> Master list combining both ideation passes. Each idea has: simple description, who buys, SAP hooks, why Sedna wins, and a leading metric.

## Pass 1 — Foundational SAP × AI plays

### 1. ClaimDesk AI — Agentic claims & appeals on SAP for insurers + Medicaid
- **Buyer:** Insurance carriers, NYS Medicaid, healthcare payers.
- **SAP:** FS-CD, FS-CM, S/4HANA Finance, GRC, BTP + AI Core, Joule, Signavio.
- **Moat:** Sedna's existing insurance + SLED footprint.
- **Metric:** 60–80% claim cycle-time reduction.

### 2. GxP Copilot for S/4HANA — Validated AI agents for life sciences
- **Buyer:** NJ/NYC pharma corridor (Merck, BMS, Pfizer, Regeneron, J&J).
- **SAP:** QM, EWM, MII, S/4 Manufacturing, MDG, GRC, AI Core (versioned), SAP Build.
- **Moat:** GAMP 5 + 21 CFR Part 11 validation pack — almost no SI ships this.
- **Metric:** 40–70% deviation cycle-time reduction.

### 3. BidBot NYC — Agentic public-sector procurement on SAP Ariba (MWBE-aware)
- **Buyer:** NYC + NYS agencies, federal contractors.
- **SAP:** Ariba Sourcing/Contracts, S/4 MM, BTP, DOX, Joule, GRC.
- **Moat:** **MWBE certification + SLED procurement fluency.**
- **Metric:** Bid response: 3 weeks → 3 days.

### 4. CloseRoom — Autonomous month-end close agents on SAP S/4
- **Buyer:** Every SAP customer; NYC public finance.
- **SAP:** S/4 Finance, Group Reporting, Cash Mgmt, BPC, Joule (existing GA agents).
- **Moat:** Fastest revenue; rides SAP's GA agents.
- **Metric:** 7-day close → 1-day close.

### 5. SchoolDesk — Agentic citizen/constituent services on SuccessFactors for SLED
- **Buyer:** NYC DOE, CUNY, MTA, NYC H+H.
- **SAP:** SuccessFactors EC + Payroll, S/4 Public Sector, SAP Build Apps, Joule, BTP.
- **Moat:** SLED + multilingual (Local Law 30) + SuccessFactors depth.
- **Metric:** First-contact resolution +40%; AHT −60%.

## Pass 2 — Hiring-focused plays

### 6. TalentMesh Global — Agentic hiring stack for big MNCs ⭐ **prototype built**
- **Buyer:** Fortune 500 on SuccessFactors (JPM, Pfizer, Merck, P&G, Verizon, Cigna).
- **SAP:** SuccessFactors Recruiting + EC + Onboarding, Joule, AI Core, BTP, Knowledge Graph.
- **Moat:** Riding May 2026 Joule HR GA + Sedna's 20-year SF depth.
- **Metric:** Time-to-fill ↓ 50%, cost-per-hire ↓ 40%.

### 7. FoundersDesk — Fractional AI recruiting pod for tech startups
- **Buyer:** YC / a16z / Techstars portfolio cos (NYC heavy).
- **SAP:** None at first; SuccessFactors upsell at 250+ employees.
- **Moat:** "Land cheap, expand-into-MNC" wedge.
- **Metric:** 4–6 hires/month per pod at $8–15K flat fee.

### 8. MedStaff AI — Agentic clinician + pharma R&D hiring
- **Buyer:** NY-Presbyterian, Mount Sinai, NYU Langone, Northwell + NYC/NJ pharma.
- **SAP:** SuccessFactors EC + Time + Workforce; S/4 Healthcare HR.
- **Moat:** Credentialing automation (NPDB, OIG, FDA debarment) + healthcare vertical depth.
- **Metric:** Travel-nurse spend −30–50%; credentialing 21 → 3 days.

### 9. CivicHire NYC — MWBE-aware public-sector hiring on SAP
- **Buyer:** NYC DCAS, DOE, MTA, Port Authority, NYS OGS.
- **SAP:** SuccessFactors + S/4 Public Sector + GRC.
- **Moat:** **Most defensible play in the deck** — MWBE + SLED + LL144 + LL30 + civil-service rules.
- **Metric:** Time-to-hire 6 mo → 6 weeks.

### 10. FrontlineHire — High-volume hourly hiring for retail/hospitality/logistics/manufacturing
- **Buyer:** Walmart, Target, Marriott, Hilton, FedEx, UPS, Tier-1 manufacturers.
- **SAP:** SuccessFactors EC + Time + Workforce Software.
- **Moat:** SuccessFactors Time/Payroll/Attendance is already a stated Sedna strength.
- **Metric:** Hire-to-shift in 24 hrs; 90-day attrition −30%.

### 11. AuditReady AEDT — Independent LL144 / AIVIA / CO AI Act bias-audit-as-a-service ⭐ **prototype built**
- **Buyer:** Every NYC employer using AI in hiring; spreads to IL, CO, CA, EU.
- **SAP:** Connector layer to SuccessFactors + non-SAP ATSes.
- **Moat:** **Auditor independence + MWBE + AI Governance practice + recurring revenue.** This is the regulatory tollbooth.
- **Metric:** $25K–$150K per AEDT, per year, recurring.

## Why this prototype combines #6 and #11

- They share the same backend (SuccessFactors + AI Core + tamper-evident logs).
- Customers buy them together: the hiring platform creates the audit obligation; the audit creates the customer's defense.
- Selling one is selling both — every TalentMesh deal includes an annual audit retainer; every standalone audit creates pull-through to TalentMesh.
- Demonstrates Sedna's full stack: **implement the SAP system + apply AI agents + own the AI governance** — all three Sedna service lines visible in one product.
